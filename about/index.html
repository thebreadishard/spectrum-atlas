About

Spectrum Atlas is the public front door of one research pipeline: infrared spectra of molecules, corrected by a learned layer that is trained on coupled-cluster anchors, with the status and the provenance of every number. It is built as a capstone project and documented in the open.

Licences

Data: CC BY 4.0. Code: MIT. Reading, browsing and downloading need no account; sign-in will be asked only of people who want to feed the pipeline (request a run, submit a molecule).
