Methods

The pipeline computes a cheap harmonic spectrum for a molecule, predicts the correction that a coupled-cluster calculation would make to it, and licenses that prediction per family of vibrations against measured anchors. Everything below links to the pre-registration or the note that carries it.

Three lessons

  1. Every derived quantity gets a second route. Finite-difference quartics turned out to be noise; a benzene Hessian in the corpus was wrong by 133 cm⁻¹ because of a quadrature grid, found by an analytic second calculation. Quality policy.
  2. The couplings are learned only in local coordinates. In the basis of normal modes the correction's couplings are unlearnable at any data size; written as pairwise terms in bonds and angles the same data teach them. E7.
  3. The coupled-cluster correction is local too, one bond further. Benzene's CCSD(T) correction sits 98 % inside the pairwise pattern once pairs two bonds apart are included. E8.
