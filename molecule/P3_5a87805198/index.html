How well is this spectrum known?

The spectra the pipeline prints come from the release networks of ΔH network v1.4, trained on all 1132 labelled entries of the training pool (an entry is one corpus row: a labelled molecule in one charge state; 4 molecules appear in two layers), test sets (a) and (b) included: each of the 3 networks is fitted on a random 85 % of the molecules and stopped on the other 15 %, a different 15 % per seed, so not every molecule is fitted by every network (with 3 networks about 0.15³ ≈ 0.3 % by none).
